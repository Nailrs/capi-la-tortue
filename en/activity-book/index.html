Frequently asked questions

How do I get the activity book?

It is a PDF file to download right after payment. You print it at home. Two files are included: US Letter and A4.

How many times can I print it?

As many times as you like for your family or your classroom. Reselling is not allowed.

What age is it for?

Ages 3 to 7. Each activity comes in two levels: one star for ages 3-4, two stars for ages 5-7.

Does my child need to know French?

No. All instructions are in English. The French words always come with a picture and an English translation.

Do I need a color printer?

No. Everything prints in black and white; only the cover and the color examples use color, and they are optional.
