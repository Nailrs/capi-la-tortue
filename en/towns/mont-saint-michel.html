Did you know?

  • Mont-Saint-Michel is a big rock in Normandy with an abbey at the very top.
  • Twice a day, the sea goes far away and then comes back: that is the tide, “la marée” in French.
  • When the sea is very high, the Mont is surrounded by water, like an island.

French word of the day: la marée

la marée means the tide. Say it like this: lah mah-RAY.
In the episode, Capi has children repeat it twice: “Répète avec moi : la marée !”

Capi's friend: Léon

“Moi, c'est Léon !” means “I'm Léon!”. Léon, the lamb from Mont-Saint-Michel, guides Capi and Plume. The lesson of this stop: patience.

3 questions to ask your child

  1. Do you remember the French word? Point to it in the picture and say “la marée”.
  2. Who is Léon? What animal is it?
  3. What did Plume learn? (patience)
