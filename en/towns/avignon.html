Did you know?

  • The bridge of Avignon stops in the middle of the river Rhône: only 4 arches are left.
  • A famous French song is about it: “Sur le pont d'Avignon”.
  • In Provençal, “Adessias” means “goodbye”.

French word of the day: le pont

le pont means the bridge. Say it like this: luh POHN.
In the episode, Capi has children repeat it twice: “Répète avec moi : le pont !”

Capi's friend: Marius

“Moi, c'est Marius !” means “I'm Marius!”. Marius, the beaver from Avignon, guides Capi and Plume. The lesson of this stop: saying sorry.

3 questions to ask your child

  1. Do you remember the French word? Point to it in the picture and say “le pont”.
  2. Who is Marius? What animal is it?
  3. What did Plume learn? (saying sorry)
