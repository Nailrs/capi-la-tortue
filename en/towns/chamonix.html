Chamonix for kids: coloring page and French word

Stop 13 of Capi the Turtle's tour of France. In Chamonix, at the foot of Mont Blanc, everything is covered in snow. Tonin the little chamois reminds us that in the mountains, we wait for those who go more slowly.

Did you know?

  • Chamonix is at the foot of Mont Blanc, the highest mountain in the Alps.
  • Mont Blanc is about 4,800 metres (15,700 feet) high.
  • The very first Winter Olympic Games took place in Chamonix, in 1924.

French word of the day: la neige

la neige means the snow. Say it like this: lah NEZH.
In the episode, Capi has children repeat it twice: “Répète avec moi : la neige !”

Capi's friend: Tonin

“Moi, c'est Tonin !” means “I'm Tonin!”. Tonin, the chamois from Chamonix, guides Capi and Plume. The lesson of this stop: waiting for those who go more slowly.

3 questions to ask your child

  1. Do you remember the French word? Point to it in the picture and say “la neige”.
  2. Who is Tonin? What animal is it?
  3. What did Plume learn? (waiting for those who go more slowly)
